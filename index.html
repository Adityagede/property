Can you support a property before it is ready to rent?+

Yes. We can discuss launch preparation, presentation, operational requirements and a practical plan for getting your property guest-ready.

Will I receive updates on my property's performance?+

Clear communication is central to our approach. We can agree on a reporting scope and frequency that match the level of service you choose.

Do you work across all areas in Bali?+

Our initial concept focuses on Canggu, Umalas and Uluwatu. Please reach out with your property's location so we can discuss the available service coverage.

Every property is different. Following an initial conversation, the management scope, fees and responsibilities should be clearly documented in a tailored agreement.
